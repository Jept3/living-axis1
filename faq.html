FAQ

Clarity before care.

A clean FAQ structure for the questions most likely to affect confidence and booking.

What is The Living Axis?

A therapeutic wellness brand centered on recovery, mobility, nervous system care and restorative touch. It is intentionally positioned differently from a conventional spa or massage brand.

Do you offer mobile or private sessions?

Yes—the brand brief includes private/mobile sessions. Exact travel radius, locations and fees still need to be confirmed by the business.

How do I book?

The final site can connect directly to the client’s preferred scheduling platform. Until that URL is provided, the booking page functions as a branded placeholder.

What should I expect during a session?

The service is positioned around therapeutic attention, recovery and restoration. Final intake, session flow and contraindication language should be provided by the practitioner.

What is your cancellation policy?

The policies page is already structured for this information, but the exact cancellation window and fees should be supplied by the business before launch.

Will The Living Axis sell products?

Not yet. The site is intentionally structured so apparel, recovery tools and wellness products can eventually live under the same master brand.
